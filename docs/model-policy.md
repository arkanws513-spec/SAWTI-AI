# Model and data policy

- Use your own voice or voices with explicit permission covering the intended training and generation uses.
- Do not train on scraped voice recordings or datasets with unclear rights.
- Do not imitate a real person in a way that could mislead, defraud, harass, or falsely attribute statements.
- Label generated audio as AI-generated when sharing it in contexts where listeners could reasonably mistake it for an authentic recording.
- Review the license of every dataset, codebase, pretrained checkpoint, and model weight independently.
