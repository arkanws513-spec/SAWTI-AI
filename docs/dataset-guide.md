# Recording and dataset guide

For the first experiments, record your own voice in a quiet room using a consistent microphone distance and level. Avoid music, reverb, background conversations, and aggressive noise reduction.

Suggested starting collection:
- Clear conversational Arabic across varied sentences.
- Modern Standard Arabic and the dialect(s) you actually intend to use.
- A range of natural speaking pace and intonation.
- Separate singing recordings only if you intend to research singing; ordinary speech alone is not a reliable singing dataset.

Keep a private log with recording date, language/dialect, content description, sample rate, and consent/provenance. Do not publish raw voice recordings in this repository.

The current validator accepts WAV and flags clips outside 1–20 seconds, non-mono files, sample rates other than 24 kHz, and possible clipping. These are advisory checks, not a guarantee of training quality.
